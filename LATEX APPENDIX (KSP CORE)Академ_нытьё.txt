\section{Minimal KSP Core (Informational Specification)}

This appendix contains a compact representation of the minimal
set of generators and relations underlying the KSP framework.

The purpose of this section is not to assert physical validity,
but to provide a structured encoding of the system logic.

\subsection{Root Constants}

\begin{align}
A &= 144 \\
T &= 367 \\
G_0 &= 7 \\
C &= \frac{1}{720} \\
\varphi &= \frac{1 + \sqrt{5}}{2} \\
R &= 31^2 \\
\sqrt{5} &\text{ (auxiliary scaling)}
\end{align}

\subsection{Geometric Constant}

\begin{equation}
G_K = \frac{31^2 \sqrt{5}}{\varphi^{12} \cdot 10^{11}}
\end{equation}

\subsection{Scaling Relations}

\begin{align}
\frac{\varphi^{12}}{\sqrt{5}} &\approx 144 + \frac{1}{720} \\
10^{11} &\approx (\varphi^6 \cdot 31)^4
\end{align}

\subsection{General Reconstruction Scheme}

\begin{itemize}
\item Root constants define geometric structure
\item Geometric structure defines $G_K$
\item $G_K$ defines scale relations
\item Scale relations define derived quantities
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Remarks}

The representation above summarizes the minimal algebraic structure
used in the KSP framework.

Additional layers (astronomical, biological, or symbolic)
are not required for internal consistency and are therefore omitted
from the core formulation.
